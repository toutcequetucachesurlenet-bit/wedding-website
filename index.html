The Venue

Villa del Balbianello • Lake Como, Italy

Getting There & Travel Information

Villa del Balbianello is situated on the Lenno peninsula of Lake Como. The venue is accessible primarily via private water taxis from Lenno or Como Town.

Recommended Airports

  • Milan Malpensa (MXP): 50 minutes drive or train connection to Como.
  • Milan Linate (LIN): 1 hour 15 minutes drive to Lake Como.

Accommodations

We have arranged room blocks at local partner hotels. Please check back for booking codes or reach out to us directly for assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our weekend

Are children invited to the wedding?

While we love your little ones, our wedding celebration will be an adult-only event.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to capacity limits at Villa del Balbianello, we are only able to accommodate guests listed on your official invitation.

What is the weather like in September?

Lake Como typically experiences mild, pleasant autumn weather with temperatures ranging from 18°C to 24°C (64°F to 75°F). Evenings near the lake can be cool, so a light wrap or jacket is recommended.
